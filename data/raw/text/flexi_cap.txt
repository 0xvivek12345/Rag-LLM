HDFC Flexi Cap Direct Plan Growth is an Equity Mutual Fund Scheme in the Flexi Cap category, launched by HDFC Mutual Fund. This scheme was made available to investors on 10 Dec 1999. Dhruv Muchhal is the current fund manager of HDFC Flexi Cap Direct Plan Growth, managing the fund since Jun 2023. The fund is rated Very High risk on the riskometer.

Investment Objective: The scheme seeks to generate capital appreciation / income from a portfolio, predominantly invested in equity & equity related instruments.

Fund benchmark: NIFTY 500 Total Return Index.

Fund size (AUM): Rs 1,13,606.47 Cr. Latest NAV as of 25 Sep 2026 is Rs 2,214.57.

Expense ratio of HDFC Flexi Cap Direct Plan Growth: 0.77%.

Exit load: Exit load of 1% if redeemed within 1 year.

Minimum investments: Minimum for 1st investment is Rs 100. Minimum for 2nd investment is Rs 100. Minimum SIP investment is Rs 100. Minimum lumpsum investment is Rs 100.

Stamp duty on investment: 0.005% (from July 1st, 2020).

Tax implication: If you redeem within one year, returns are taxed at 20%. If you redeem after one year, returns exceeding Rs 1.25 lakh in a financial year are taxed at 12.5%.

Expense ratio definition: A fee payable to a mutual fund house for managing your mutual fund investments. It is the total percentage of a company's fund assets used for administrative, management, advertising, and other expenses.

Exit load definition: A fee payable to a mutual fund house for exiting a fund (fully or partially) before the completion of a specified period from the date of investment.

The registrar and transfer agent (RTA) for HDFC Mutual Fund is Cams; statements and tax documents such as capital gains statements can be downloaded from the CAMS website www.camsonline.com.

The Scheme Information Document (SID) for HDFC schemes is available at https://www.hdfcfund.com.
